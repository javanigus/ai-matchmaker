# 2026-07-31 — Positioning, differentiators, monetization, platform

## What changed

**vision.md**
- Added a "Positioning" section: internally described as "an AI Matchmaker with a dating marketplace" — the AI Matchmaker is the product, the marketplace is how users meet people; not a support chatbot or generic assistant.
- Added a "Core Differentiators" section: conversational onboarding, the evolving Compatibility Graph, explained compatibility scores, and a persistent AI Matchmaker so users are never just waiting for matches.
- Added a Guiding Principle: the AI Matchmaker is the single interface for the dating experience — every meaningful interaction should be reachable through conversation with it.

**prd.md**
- Expanded the AI Matchmaker section with its concrete capabilities (answer questions, discuss matches, explain recommendations, help with profile/photos, help users understand themselves) and the "never feel abandoned while waiting for matches" commitment.
- Added a "Platform" section: web and mobile as first-class experiences, with desktop suited to onboarding/long conversations/photo review/profile management and mobile suited to browsing/messaging/voice/notifications.
- Filled in "Monetization": tiered subscriptions, higher tiers unlock more AI capability/usage, explicitly no boosts/super likes/paid visibility/artificial scarcity. Left tier/pricing specifics as an open question.

## Why it changed

Founder accepted these as Version 1 decisions: a clear internal positioning statement, the reasons a user would choose this over a traditional dating app, a monetization model that stays consistent with the "AI works for the user, not engagement" principle, and a hybrid web/mobile platform split by use case.
