Privacy Policy

Last updated August 1, 2026 · Placeholder text for product planning purposes

1. What we collect

We collect what you tell your AI Matchmaker directly (onboarding conversation, ongoing chats, profile text), photos you upload (both public Profile Photos and private Learning Photos), and behavioral signals from how you use the product (likes, passes, saves, and any feedback you choose to give). Standard account information — name, email, birthday, phone — is collected at signup.

2. How your Compatibility Graph is used

Everything we learn about you is organized into your Compatibility Graph, which powers your recommendations and compatibility explanations. Your Compatibility Graph and AI Memory are private to you — other users never see them directly. They only see your public profile and any Compatibility Report you choose to generate and share context for.

3. Learning Photos stay private

Learning Photos are used only to help your AI Matchmaker understand you — they are never shown publicly, never shared with other users, and never used to auto-populate your public profile without your explicit choice.

4. Improving recommendations

If you leave "Improve recommendations" turned on in Settings, anonymized patterns from your feedback may help refine matching quality for other users. This never includes your identity, your Compatibility Graph, or anything personally identifying. You can turn this off at any time.

5. Data retention and deletion

If you delete your account, your Compatibility Graph, AI Memory, matches, and message history are permanently deleted. We retain minimal billing records as required by law. See Delete Account in Settings for the full list of what's removed.

6. Your controls

You can review, correct, or remove anything in your Compatibility Graph at any time. You control your profile visibility, whether your distance and city are shown, and whether you appear in Search at all, from Settings → Privacy.
