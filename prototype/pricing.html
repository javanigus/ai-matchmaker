Questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes — upgrade or downgrade anytime from Settings. Changes take effect at your next billing cycle.

Do you sell visibility boosts or super likes?

No, and we don't plan to. It undermines trust in the product, and it's not how we think matching should work.

What happens to my data if I cancel?

You keep your account and My Profile on the Free plan. Nothing is deleted unless you delete your account.
