Frequently asked

Is my Compatibility Graph private?

Yes. Only you can see your full Compatibility Graph and AI Memory — matches only see your public profile and any Compatibility Report you choose to generate.

Can I pause my profile without deleting my account?

Yes — use "Hide from Search" in Settings, or reach out and we'll pause it for you while keeping everything intact.

How do I report a safety concern?

Use the "Account or safety concern" topic above, or email us directly — these are reviewed with priority.
