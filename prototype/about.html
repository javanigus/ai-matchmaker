Why AI Matchmaker exists.

We think dating apps optimized for the wrong thing — engagement, not outcomes. We're building something different.

Traditional dating apps have a broken incentive

Apps that make money from engagement are quietly incentivized to keep you swiping, not to get you off the app and into a relationship. Endless feeds, paid boosts, and manufactured scarcity all optimize for time spent, not for finding the right person. We don't think that's an accident, and we don't think it's fixable from inside that model.

Conversation beats forms

A 40-field onboarding form gets you dishonest, low-effort answers. A real conversation — the kind a good friend or matchmaker would have with you — surfaces what actually matters: how you talk about your family, what you get excited about, what you're avoiding. Your AI Matchmaker interviews you the way a person would, not a spreadsheet.

The Compatibility Graph

Everything your AI Matchmaker learns about you — facts, preferences, inferred values, and how confident it is in each — lives in one evolving Compatibility Graph. It's the source of truth behind every recommendation and every compatibility explanation you see. Matching happens by reasoning over this graph, not by filtering a checklist of profile fields.

Transparency, not a black box

You should never have to wonder why the AI believes something about you. Your Compatibility Graph shows what it currently believes; your AI Memory shows why — every conversation, photo, and correction that shaped it, in order. Nothing is hidden, and anything you disagree with, you can correct or remove.

You're always in control

The AI recommends — it never decides for you. It suggests which photos to lead with, drafts profile text, and explains its reasoning, but you make every public-facing decision. Passing, liking, and saving are always yours to make, and always reversible.
